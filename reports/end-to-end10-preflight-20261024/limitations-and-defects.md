# Confirmed limitations and diagnostics before paid execution

1. **The production field mapper is local.** Optional DeepSeek calls perform all-span section placement and advisory schema audit. They do not directly produce the final typed field values. Repeating a deterministic field mapper will not necessarily repair a missing field.
2. **Search scope is per resume.** Field paths and record filters exist, but no cross-candidate persistence/index/combination-query service was found. Candidate-search readiness is not established.
3. **Semantic labels are partial.** v5 has 17 expected fields: 11 saved explicit Group01/02 decisions plus six older Manjunath expectations. A069 location is explicitly uncertain. Remaining 218 worksheet rows are pending. Do not infer negatives or precision/recall from positive partial labels. All submitted reviewer names are self-declared and retained as recorded; the importer does not authenticate identity or perform a fresh visual PDF review.
4. **Recovery is bounded, not universal.** Local recovery reruns detection only when unresolved text yields title-style cuts. Feedback can revisit previously assigned spans; there is no semantic lock on correct placements. Source conservation does not imply record/section correctness. Stagnation fingerprint does not explicitly include hierarchical field output.
5. **Saved grouping stress cases.** Bulk baselines report Siva Mari: 106 experience records; Divya Balyan: 34 education records; Vikrant Roy: 126 project records. These counts are suspicious fragmentation diagnostics, not confirmed true job/degree/project counts. They deliberately exercise grouping limits in the proposed controlled evaluation. Human record-boundary labels are needed before scoring these associations.
6. **Extraction changes source inventories.** Exact quote/offset checks bind to finalized extracted blocks. PDF enrichment is best-effort; glyph/layout failures can be warned and retained. OCR requires dependencies and does not provide independent word-accuracy truth. Auto extraction may differ from frozen artifacts, so source-bound section labels must never be transferred by element index alone.
7. **Legacy and nested output may differ by design.** Nested shape/citation checks can withhold legacy heuristic values. Generic legacy evidence IDs do not provide the same guarantee as nested per-item exact quotes. Diagnose any disagreements separately.
8. **Sampling scope.** Two controls intentionally reuse prior LLM documents; eight generalization documents exclude inspected prior selection hashes and filenames. Conservative exclusions include previously selected documents without proof of completed requests. No claim is made about unrecorded provider history. Exact/normalized-text hashing does not detect every rewritten duplicate.
9. **Resource/provider limits.** Long resumes may exceed 600 units or 200,000 prompt characters after segmentation and be retained locally without partial provider submission. The planned 40-request cap is a ceiling, not an assurance that every document receives two complete reviews. Authentication/model validity is untested pending confirmation.

No production fixes are included in this evaluation. Recommendations are to adjudicate record-boundary stress cases, expand explicitly saved source-bound labels, compare legacy/nested semantics by field, improve hierarchy-aware progress diagnostics, and design a record-preserving candidate index in a separately authorized implementation. Do not convert these diagnostics into fabricated ground truth.
