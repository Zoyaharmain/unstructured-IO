## Issues fixed in this pass

**Scope accounting in reports:** the latest 100 report's 417 meaningful pending spans combine **413 spans in eight out-of-scope documents** and **four in 92 candidate/unknown-scope documents**. Added explicit scope-separated totals to the reporting code and generated a [corrected separate report](latest-100-corrected/report.md). Original reports remain preserved. The four pending source spans are an editing instruction in Ankit, an Additional Information heading in Jasmin, two backslash characters in Khalid, and an HRJ tracking marker in Nimisha. They remain visible until their treatment is source-supported; they were not forced into resume sections.

**Repeat selection:** the default `run_pilot50_review.py --count 300` always includes the original 15 and draws from an existing pool; it does not automatically exclude prior batches. The old additional-100 selector excludes only the specified prior manifest, so excluding the 250 alone can reselect the later 100. Added [select_unseen_resumes.py](../../scripts/select_unseen_resumes.py): it unions all saved batch manifests, excludes both hashes and filenames, verifies original source hashes, deduplicates aliases, uses deterministic strata, refuses insufficient candidates/corrupt history, and refuses output replacement. Excluding hashes prevents renamed copies of a processed PDF from returning; filename exclusion is an additional conservative safeguard.

Prepared [selection-unseen-300.json](selection-unseen-300.json): **300 unique original-source-verified PDFs, zero hash overlap and zero filename overlap with the 350 saved batch selections**. [Verification](selection-verification.json) records the hash check. This is an offline selection, not resume classification, human approval or permission to scale. It does not establish that all selected PDFs are resumes, and PDF hashes cannot detect separately encoded copies of the same candidate's resume. Selection excludes prior diagnostic batch selections; PDFs previously extracted only in the upstream local inventory remain eligible by design.

## Remaining blockers

The latest 100 still has employment association flags in 86 documents and education flags in 49, including out-of-scope files; these counts overlap and are heuristic review signals, not error rates. Examples include dozens of unlinked employment blocks in DeepaR/Dhiman, and detached/numeric education groups in PUPPYKUMARI. The role/date/employer ownership therefore cannot be certified merely because the provider supplied valid section assignments. Conservative grouping preserves uncertain content rather than inventing links.

Known source issues also remain: Gopal has damaged-text/parser disagreements, Keerthi requires an original-image/OCR reference, and some output boundaries need distinction between fused source headings and ordinary words. The provider labels sections and offsets; it does not independently approve the final deterministic job/education records. A structural pass is not proof of these relationships.

The [pilot gate](pilot-gate.json) still reports **0 of 15 required documents with qualifying human approval/export or exclusion**. No meaningful accuracy score can be established against an unapproved reference set. The [acceptance review pack](../acceptance-review-20261021/report.md) and running editor at **http://127.0.0.1:8022** are ready for that work. Known extraction defects must be corrected in a new source version and annotations rebased before approval.
