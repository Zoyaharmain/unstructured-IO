rate assignment, and debit account verification. Automated validations, real-time status updates, and exception

closures enhanced staff productivity. Seamless integration with core banking systems enabled faster and more
reliable transaction processing. Standardized workflows ensured consistency across teams and strengthened audit
readiness. Overall, the solution accelerated processing, minimized errors, boosted staff efficiency, and delivered a

Project Title:   MWS (My Work Space)
Duration:        Jan 2021-2022 June

Manual tracking of customer transactions across multiple legacy systems created inefficiencies, delays, and limited
