Website Critique Assignment
Submitted by: Sakshi Desai
Company: Eragina Digital Solutions
Website Reviewed: https://www.doright.world



1. Design Review

      The overall design of the website lacks visual appeal.
      The color scheme is dull and not vibrant, which makes the content less engaging.
      The navigation bar only has two buttons, which makes the website feel incomplete
       and confusing.
      The images used are not attractive and do not capture attention or reflect the brand
       effectively.



2. User Experience Review

      The website is simple, but it does not offer a smooth and interactive experience for
       users.
      Important sections like “About Us,” “Contact,” or “Services” are missing from the
       top menu.
      There are no clear call-to-action (CTA) buttons to guide users.
      The content feels disconnected and does not highlight the purpose of the website
       well.



3. Suggestions for Improvement

      Use a more vibrant and modern color scheme to improve visual engagement.
      Add more navigation options such as “About,” “Services,” and “Contact” for better
       usability.
      Replace current images with high-quality and brand-relevant visuals.
      Add clear CTA buttons like “Learn More,” “Get Involved,” or “Join Us” to guide
       user flow.
      Improve the homepage layout with well-defined sections and headlines to make it
       easier for visitors to understand the website’s mission.
