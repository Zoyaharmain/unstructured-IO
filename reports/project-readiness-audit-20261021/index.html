## Fixes and validation of the additional 100

Implemented strict provider error diagnostics and repair feedback without relaxing exact coverage or atomic fallback. Guards distinguish actual PDF line boundaries from unsupported word cuts. Optional provider `type` is accepted only as the exact documented `json_object` constant. Future provider requests are saved without credentials for reproducibility.

Strengthened source interval and record ownership validation; employer/education field references stay within their own record. Added source-supported employment/qualification anchors, conservative handling of shared accomplishments, and visible uncertainty for unlinked content. Readable records and structured entries use the same grouping. Reports now include unlinked-record and field-review concerns previously omitted.

Added conservative PDF text-layer glyph repair and missing-line recovery with source provenance. Recovery requires matching source evidence; ambiguous glyphs and OCR errors remain flagged. Repeated passages at different PDF locations remain preserved.

Added narrow source-artifact handling for located page-number footers such as `1 | P a g e` and standalone bullets. Meaningful text, graduation years, C#, contact information and headings remain reviewable. No source spans were discarded.

## Remaining material limitations

The provider assigns section categories and offsets. It does **not** independently verify the final employer/title/date/responsibility or degree/institution/year/grade tuples. Deterministic grouping remains provisional, even when every span is assigned. No independent human-approved evaluation set was established by this audit.

Targeted original-PDF checks confirmed a missing Associate Software Engineer title, missing paragraph words in Nageswar Reddy, and missing Year/Score table headers in Prasad Potdar. Source-supported recovery reproductions are retained in [source-repair evidence](../pipeline200-root-fix-20261021/source-repair-replay-v2.json). These are targeted results, not a fresh full-corpus extraction run.

Gopal still contains ambiguous damaged glyphs; Bevara's email differs between PDF text parsers; Keerthi's scanned page has OCR spelling and column-order errors. These need PDF adjudication or improved OCR with a new extraction version. Increasing DPI alone did not resolve Keerthi in the recorded experiment. Genuine repeated passages must not be automatically deduplicated. Fused PDF headings also need distinction from ordinary words split incorrectly.

The four failed 200-document provider responses have reproducible offset/schema defects. More precise validators and retry feedback are implemented, but their live-provider recovery has **not** been demonstrated by a new 200-document paid run. Offline acceptance of saved responses does not prove future model compliance.

## What to do next

1. Review the six meaningful spans above and the association warnings against original PDFs. Define Additional Information/professional-membership policy before changing labels.
2. Resolve the damaged-text, email-conflict and scanned-page cases with source evidence. Store any corrected extraction as a new version and rebase annotations; preserve existing inputs.
3. Complete explicit human review of the source-bound 15-document pilot, covering every page, section and relationship. The [existing review workflow](../../docs/PILOT_HUMAN_REVIEW.md) explains the editor, approval provenance and evaluation commands. Start the local editor with `.venv/bin/python scripts/review_pilot.py --port 8022`; it makes no LLM calls.
4. Run section and relationship evaluation against those independently approved references, then a held-out sample. Agree acceptance thresholds based on those results. Only then consider another bounded live batch with frozen source, prompt, model and code hashes.

**Proceed now with review and offline validation. Do not proceed with a larger paid batch or production acceptance on the current coverage figures.**
